# Mona Lisa tutorial — implementation handoff

Comic completion or Skip opens Mona Lisa training. Seven tools are explained: Check, Mark, Overview, Area, Exact, Remove and Video. Training pauses the timer and spends no energy or hint stock. Video is an explanation only, not a simulated rewarded-ad payout.

The first three targets teach the tools. A fourth, dark antique brooch, remains for independent search. Completion requires all four targets. Legacy completed Mona Lisa saves retain their collection; replay through the story starts a fresh attempt without deleting collection or best results.

Fourth target asset: `Assets/Art/Prototype/Gameplay/MonaBrooch.png`. Generated as a transparent, subdued antique bronze floral brooch with an olive centre and aged paint texture, matched to Mona Lisa. Catalog placement: UV (0.665, 0.465), width fraction 0.078, rotation -14 degrees.

Verified: script compilation and isolated static UI previews. No Play, APK build, phone testing, or player save reset was performed.
